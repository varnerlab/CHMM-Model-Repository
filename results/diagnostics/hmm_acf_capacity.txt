Reading rule (declared before the run): the estimation criterion, not the state
budget, is implicated on a band exactly when (a) ACF-targeted K = 3 error is
materially below the likelihood-fit error at both K, and (b) ACF-targeted K = 3
and K = 18 are close. If (b) fails, the state budget itself binds. These fits are
multistart heuristic optima: attained errors are upper bounds on the class's best
error (attainability certificates), not certified global minima. The K = 3 vs
K = 18 comparison is ACHIEVED ACCURACY under this declared optimizer, not a
class-level equivalence statement: the K = 18 fits stop at the iteration cap
and their stationary laws are near-degenerate, so K = 18 is the looser
certificate and no practical-equivalence margin is claimed.

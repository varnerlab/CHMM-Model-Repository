Reading (scope):
  - These are converged multistart LIKELIHOOD fits, so the comparison shows what ML
    fitting delivers at each state count, on the fit window and on a held-out window.
  - It does NOT by itself bound what the K-state class could achieve on the ACF axis;
    realizable attainability is measured by run_hmm_acf_capacity.jl (valid-HMM,
    ACF-targeted), with run_exp_mode_diagnostic.jl as an exploratory out-of-class
    curve-fit reference; the artifacts should be read together.
  - The ticker count is descriptive (no cross-sectional dependence correction; the
    panel shares market-wide factors, and SPY is included).

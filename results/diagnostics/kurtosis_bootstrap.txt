Reading:
  - At L = 10 the percentile 95% CI for the IS - OoS difference is [-4.244, 9.673],
    which covers zero: the data do not exclude equal IS and OoS excess kurtosis
    at the 5% level under this bootstrap. This is a non-rejection, not evidence
    of equality.

  - Pr(IS > OoS) at L = 10: 0.756. This is a DESCRIPTIVE bootstrap probability -
    the fraction of independent resample pairs in which the IS draw exceeds the
    OoS draw. It is NOT a p-value: each window is resampled around its own
    observed kurtosis, so no equal-kurtosis null is imposed (fourth-review item 3).

Stationary block bootstrap displays on excess kurtosis + tail-robust inference
Source data: SPY IS (T = 2516) and SPY OoS (T = 572) excess log growth
Bootstrap construction: Politis-Romano 1994, mean block length L, 5000 replicates per L.
========================================================================================

SCOPE. The observed top-5% Hill estimate on this series is ~3.15 < 4, so the
population fourth moment is plausibly infinite. In that regime raw excess kurtosis
has no stable population value and the n-out-of-n percentile bootstrap is not a
calibrated interval for it. Sections [D1]-[D2] below are therefore DESCRIPTIVE
sensitivity displays. Section [I] is the inferential statement: winsorized excess
kurtosis at trim q (tails clamped at the q / 1-q empirical quantiles, thresholds
recomputed inside every resample) has finite moments of all orders for any tail
index, and the stationary block bootstrap is standard for such smooth marginal
functionals under mixing.

Reading:
  - Inference (winsorized q = 0.01, L = 10): the 95% percentile CI for the IS - OoS
    difference is [-1.262, 1.827], which covers zero: the data do not exclude equal
    IS and OoS winsorized excess kurtosis at the 5% level. Non-rejection, not
    equality.
  - The raw-kurtosis displays [D1]-[D2] are descriptive only; under the paper's own
    heavy-tail diagnosis (Hill < 4) they cannot be read as calibrated 95% intervals.
  - Pr(IS > OoS) at L = 10 (raw): 0.756. This is a DESCRIPTIVE bootstrap probability -
    the fraction of independent resample pairs in which the IS draw exceeds the
    OoS draw. It is NOT a p-value: each window is resampled around its own
    observed kurtosis, so no equal-kurtosis null is imposed.

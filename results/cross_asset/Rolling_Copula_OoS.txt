==============================================================================================================
Paired static-vs-rolling Student-t copula comparison, six-asset SPY cross-section (third-review item 2)
==============================================================================================================

Setup:
  - Universe: SPY, NVDA, JNJ, JPM, AAPL, QQQ
  - Per-asset CHMM-N marginals at K = 3 (paper headline K*), fitted once on IS, held fixed in BOTH arms.
  - Quarter partition: 10 OoS blocks of 63 days; the final 5-day partial block is included.
  - STATIC arm: copula fit once on IS; per-quarter simulation scored on that quarter's realised matrix.
  - ROLLING arm: copula refit each quarter on the trailing 252 days; scored on the SAME quarter target.
  - Paths per simulation: 200.  Per-quarter seeds shared across arms (seed-level CRN).
  - Seed root: 20260422

Headline (paired across 10 quarters):
  Mean per-quarter off-diag MAE, static copula   : 0.2894
  Mean per-quarter off-diag MAE, rolling refit   : 0.2232
  Mean paired difference (static - rolling)      : 0.0662  (sd 0.0704, se 0.0223)
  Paired t (9 df)                        : t = 2.97,  two-sided p = 0.0157
  95% t-interval for the difference              : [0.0158, 0.1166]
  Quarters favouring the rolling refit           : 7 / 10

Notes:
  - Both arms score the identical per-quarter targets with identical marginals and horizons, so the
    paired difference isolates the copula refit; quarter-to-quarter target difficulty cancels in d_q.
  - The CHMM-N marginals are not refit in either arm; only the dependence layer moves.
  - Uncertainty is across quarters (paired t over 10 blocks). Monte-Carlo path noise is secondary
    and partially cancelled by the shared per-quarter seeds.
  - The rolling refit can move ν* across the discrete grid {2,3,4,5,6,8,10,15,20,30} per quarter.

Setup    : SPY daily log excess growth; IS n = 2516, OoS T = 572; seed = 20260420; K* = 3.
Rows     : CHMM-N, CHMM-t (penalised ECM, lambda = 20.0), CHMM-L, CHMM-GED (per-cell reseed:
           SEED fit / SEED+1 simulation, as run_kstar3_headline.jl), and shared-nu CHMM-t
           (library baum_welch_student_t_shared_nu, nu bounds (2.1, 50.0), own seed convention).
Ensemble : one 1000-path unconditional (marginal-predictive) simulation bundle per model.
CRPS     : unbiased sample CRPS via the sorted-ensemble identity (sec:crps_methods),
           CRPS_t = (1/N) sum_i |x_i - y_t| - (1/(N(N-1))) sum_{i<j} |x_i - x_j|.
DM       : Newey-West HAC variance, Bartlett kernel, bandwidth h = floor(T^(1/3)) = 8;
           two-sided p-values under the standard-normal null; Holm step-down over the 10 pairs.
